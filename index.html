That rail at the edge of this page is Pulse, drawn the way the app draws it, with sample numbers.

Every limit, at the edge of your screen.

Each ring's outer arc is the account's leading window, usually the 5-hour session, and the inner arc the next, usually the weekly limit. Click a ring for that account's windows and reset times. Usage refreshes every 2 minutes.

  • 98%, 99%, 100%. The ring gets a red badge and an alert stays in the middle of the screen until you dismiss it.
  • Per-model caps fill the disc behind the logo as they're used.
  • Show what's left turns the rings around, so they empty as you use them.
  • Any screen edge. Drag the rail anywhere; top and bottom lay out horizontally.

A Codex pet you can talk to.

Click the pet to chat by text, or talk to it live. It runs your own Codex sign-in, keeps one conversation across restarts, and hands bigger jobs to Codex helpers while you keep talking.

  • Voice: the waveform button under the pet, or Command-click the pet.
  • ⌃ ⌥ Space opens the pet hands-free. Hold it to talk.
  • Your call: requests to act outside its folder come to you as a permission dialog.

Time for a break.

Halloween: the promo plays here this week.

Breaks keep to their times: by default a 20-second micro break every 20 minutes and a 5-minute break every hour. Thirty seconds before, a countdown grows out of the rail. The break covers every display, waits during calls, and time away counts as a break. On Halloween and Thanksgiving a short film plays behind the countdown.
