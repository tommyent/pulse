Pulse now updates itself, and its rail gets out of the way.

- **Updates in the app.** Pulse checks for new versions with Sparkle. On its second launch it asks whether to check automatically, and **Check for Updates…** in the menu bar popover checks at any time. Each update is signed, and Sparkle checks that signature against a key built into Pulse before installing it. If you have 0.2.0, this is the last version you download by hand.
- **Flick the rail.** Drag the rail to any edge and let go, or flick it toward one: it glides there. At the top and bottom the rings line up in a row.
- **Tuck it away.** Push the rail into its own edge, or into the menu bar or the Dock, and let go. It tucks into a thin sliver with a coloured tick for each account, longer the fuller its ring and red near a limit. Click the sliver to bring the rail back, or drag it out to any edge. **Minimize the rings to their edge** in the menu bar popover does the same, and a tucked rail stays tucked after a restart. With Reduce Motion on, the rail moves without gliding.

For Apple silicon Macs on macOS 26 or later. To check the download, run `shasum -a 256 -c Pulse-0.3.0.dmg.sha256` in the folder that holds both files.
